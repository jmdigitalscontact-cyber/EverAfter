FAQ

Copied from the live product — customized per couple.

How do I RSVP?

Use the personal link or QR on your invitation. The public site stays locked until then.

Can I bring a plus one?

Only names printed on your invitation can be confirmed. Capacity is reserved in advance.

Where do I sit?

Seating is assigned. At the reception, scan the venue QR and search your name — no queue at registration.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Keep phones silent and stay seated. Photos are welcome after the ceremony and throughout the reception. Guest POVs upload to the live gallery on the night.

Do you have a gift registry?

No registry. Your presence is the gift; monetary blessings can be sent from the reception app.
