What we offer

Everything a couple actually needs.

Invitation

A site that feels like the day

Hero, live countdown, venues with maps, story, gallery marquee, attire, program, entourage book, FAQ, ambient audio.

RSVP

Invitation-only replies

Unique QR per household. Tick who is coming. Dietary notes. One guarded update. Guest cap on the dashboard.

Admin

Headcount without a spreadsheet

Invitations, unused slots, seating, CSV and Google Sheets, POV ZIP, reception QR, private guest notes.

Vote

Team Bride or Team Groom

Venue QR lock, then welcome, then a required vote. One locked vote per phone. Live scores for guests and admin.

Seating

Find your seat, not the queue

Name search reveals table and seat. Floor plan pinch-zooms. Registration table stays calm.

Live gallery

Guest POVs on every phone — and the wall

Camera or roll, on-phone WebP compress, likes, notes for the couple, ZIP download, optional TV wall.
