EverAfter — Digital Wedding Suite

A complete digital wedding system: invitation website, private QR RSVP, couple admin, and a live reception app. Customized for each couple and delivered finished — not a template they have to figure out.

One-line pitch

From the first click on the invitation to the last photo on the dance floor, guests have one beautiful, private digital home for your wedding.

Who it is for

  • Invitation-only weddings with a hard guest cap
  • Church + reception days that need directions, attire, and program in one place
  • Philippine celebrations with entourage, sponsors, and assigned seating
  • Couples who want QR codes on printed invites, not public RSVP links
  • Receptions that need find-your-seat, menu, gifts, and a live guest photo wall

The guest journey

  1. They open a cinematic invitation site (story, venues, attire, program, FAQ, gallery).
  2. They RSVP only with their personal QR or link. Names are already on the card.
  3. You watch headcount, seating, and photos in one dashboard.
  4. At the venue they scan once, pick Team Bride or Team Groom, find their seat, share POVs, and see gift details.

What guests receive

Invitation website

Mobile-first, customized with names, photos, palette, and copy. Typical sections: hero and countdown, ceremony and reception with maps, love story, gallery, guest notes, attire, program, entourage book, FAQ, and invitation-only RSVP.

Private QR RSVP

Unique QR and link per household. Tick who is coming. Dietary notes. One controlled update after the first submit. No plus-ones unless you listed them. Guest cap tracked in admin.

Reception night app

Unlocked only by the venue QR. Path: Lock screen → Welcome → required Team Bride / Team Groom vote (one locked vote per phone, live scores) → find your seat → floor plan → menu with dietary filters → live POV gallery (camera or camera roll, compressed on the phone) → private note for the couple → gifts. Optional TV wall plays guest photos for the room.

Design looks

Each couple gets a finished visual identity. The sales mockup currently includes five palettes you can walk through on a call:

  • Sage Garden — forest green, cream, champagne (garden / Tagaytay)
  • Champagne Blush — dusty rose, ivory, gold (ballroom)
  • Midnight Velvet — navy, ivory, antique gold (cathedral evening)
  • Terracotta Olive — clay, olive, cream (hacienda / outdoor)
  • Ivory & Ink — charcoal, cream, gold italic (editorial city)

Photography, fonts, and copy are still customized. The look is a starting conversation, not a locked template.

What the couple receives

  • Dashboard — capacity, sent, responded, confirmed, declined, unused slots
  • Invitations — create one or bulk CSV, generate QRs, send, edit
  • Responses — names, notes, one-time edit
  • Export — CSV and Google Sheets
  • Seating — assign tables and seats
  • Photos — moderate, hide, delete, download a ZIP of every POV
  • Reception — print the venue QR, watch the vote, reset after rehearsal
  • Notes — private messages guests leave for the couple; CSV export

Security included: invitation-only RSVP, reception key in the venue QR, admin login with CSRF protection, hashed passwords, and rate limiting.

What we customize per couple

Names, date, venues, maps, palette, photography, copy, guest list and QRs, floor plan, menu, gift details, guest cap, RSVP deadline, and the night-of QR. Optional: entourage book, ambient audio, live photo wall, extra language, bulk import, planner walkthrough.

How delivery works

  1. Kickoff — names, date, venues, guest cap, photos, palette, gifts
  2. Guest list — spreadsheet of households for QR invitations
  3. Design pass — private preview of the invitation site
  4. RSVP + admin — QRs generated, dashboard walkthrough
  5. Reception pack — menu, floor, gifts, venue QR, vote + gallery
  6. Launch — printed QRs, deadline, night-of notes

Hosting is ordinary PHP + MySQL shared hosting / cPanel. Guests use the browser. No app store.
